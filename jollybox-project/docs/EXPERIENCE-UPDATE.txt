WHAT CHANGED
- Before December, the advent calendar waits behind a countdown. Christmas day is also counted down.
- Door dates change at 00:01 GMT. Test 1 December 00:00 and 00:01 using Grown-up testing.
- All-unlocked testing stays separate from the child's real door progress. Date simulation is session-only.
- Thinner arcade cabinet frames leave more space for the game.
- Slow-rise foam now compresses deeply and can be pulled out of shape, then recovers gradually.
- Rainbow sand reveals coloured grooves; “Shake the sand” clears the tray.
- Deeper, richer materials throughout the workshop; a detailed fish-free moonlit riverbed and finer interactive refraction in the pond.
- Double-tap zoom, text selection and touch callouts are suppressed during play.
- Ice climbing has narrower platforms, higher gaps and faster melting.
- Five distinct surprise openings: parcel, scroll, cracker, arcade cabinet and sealed mission.
- Reopened doors show a pictorial treasure desk. Remaining surprises stay wrapped.
- Three baby companions: Pip, Ember and Juniper. Clover's gift slot is now shape-keeping putty. Existing child surprise orders remain intact.
